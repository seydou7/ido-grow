# 📊 01 — Sénégal Job Market Overview

> **Last updated:** 2026-03-31
> **Source synthesis:** World Bank Sénégal data, ANSD (Agence Nationale de la Statistique et de la Démographie), AfDB reports, local recruiter interviews (synthesized).

---

## 🌍 Macro-Economic Context

| Indicator | Value (2024-2025) |
|---|---|
| **GDP Growth** | ~8-9% (driven by oil & gas sector launch) |
| **Working Population** | ~9 million active workers |
| **Youth Unemployment** | ~15-18% (urban areas higher) |
| **Formal Sector** | ~30% of employment (informal is dominant) |
| **Main Economic Hubs** | Dakar (dominant), Thiès, Kaolack, Saint-Louis |
| **Currency** | CFA Franc (XOF) — pegged to EUR |
| **Language of Business** | French (primary), Wolof (workplace informal) |

---

## 🚀 Major Economic Shifts (2024-2026)

### 🛢️ Oil & Gas Boom
- Sangomar oil field production started 2024 (Woodside Energy)
- GTA gas project (Mauritanie-Sénégal) — massive job creation expected
- Demand for: engineers, HSE specialists, finance, logistics, project management
- International firms entering: Total Energies, BP, Kosmos Energy

### 🏗️ Infrastructure Push (Plan Sénégal Emergent)
- Public works, BTP sector expansion
- Smart city projects in Diamniadio
- Highway and rail development

### 💻 Digital Economy Growth
- Dakar positioning as West African tech hub
- Free Zones (FONSIS) attracting tech companies
- Startup ecosystem growing: Yakhni, Gainde 2000, Wave, Orange Money

### 🤝 International Organizations Presence
- UN agencies (UNDP, UNICEF, UNFPA, WFP) — major employers of qualified professionals
- World Bank, AfDB regional offices
- NGOs (Plan International, Save the Children, Catholic Relief Services)

|---|---|---|

| **BAC+2 (BTS/DUT)** | Accounting, IT support, marketing | 150,000–250,000 XOF/month |
| **BAC+3 (Licence)** | Mid-level management, specialized roles | 250,000–400,000 XOF/month |
| **BAC+5 (Master)** | Senior management, technical specialists | 400,000–800,000 XOF/month |

## 🏢 Top Employers in Sénégal

### Private Corporate
- **Telecom**: Orange Sénégal, Free Sénégal, Expresso
- **Banking**: CBAO, Ecobank, BICIS, BDK, Coris Bank
- **Insurance**: Sénégalaise des Assurances, Allianz Sénégal
- **FMCG**: Nestlé, Unilever, Diageo (Brasseries)
- **Oil & Gas**: Total Energies, Woodside, Petrosen
- **Pharma**: Synapse Center, LABOREX Sénégal

### Public Sector
- Administration centrale (Ministères)
- Collectivités locales (Mairies)
- Entreprises parapubliques (SENELEC, SONATEL, DDD)

### International
- Ambassades et consulats
- Agences ONU
- Banques de développement

## 📱 Digital Adoption Context

- **Smartphone penetration**: ~70% in urban areas
- **Internet access**: 50-60% nationally, 85%+ in Dakar
- **Social media**: Facebook dominant, LinkedIn growing rapidly for professionals
- **Mobile money**: Wave, Orange Money — ubiquitous
- **CV submission**: Email still dominant, online portals growing
