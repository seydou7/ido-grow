---
name: ido-ui-review
description: Perform a UX/UI design, accessibility, and visual review of frontend components.
---

Perform a frontend design review:
1. Invoke the `mariama` custom subagent to inspect visual hierarchy, spacing, typography, responsiveness, and aesthetic quality.
2. If frontend implementation/fixes are needed: invoke the `fatou` custom subagent.
3. Invoke the `malick` custom subagent to validate frontend tests and accessibility.
Do not simulate agents in the parent context.
