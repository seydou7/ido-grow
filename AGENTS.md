# Agent Instructions

Before making significant changes, agents must read:

- `project-memory/PROJECT_CHARTER.md`
- `project-memory/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`
- `project-memory/CURRENT_STATE.md`
- Relevant entries in `project-memory/DECISION_LOG.md`

Important architectural or product decisions must be recorded in `project-memory/DECISION_LOG.md`.

Never overwrite or revise a previous decision. Record a new decision that explicitly supersedes the earlier one.
